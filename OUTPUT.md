# Research Outputs & Visualizations

This directory contains the primary graphical and tabular outputs generated by the reproducible analytical pipeline for the **Natural Gas Storage and TTF Price Relationship Analysis**.

---

## 1. Visual Research Artifacts

### Figure 1: TTF Wholesale Price vs. EU Gas Storage Fill Level (2016–2024)
*Dual-axis historical trajectory highlighting the 2021–2023 Energy Crisis Regime, seasonal injection/withdrawal oscillations, and statutory European Union 80% and 90% storage refill targets.*

![Figure 1: TTF Wholesale Price vs EU Gas Storage](output/fig1_ttf_storage_trajectory.png)

---

### Figure 2: Latent Regime Identification via Hamilton Markov Switching
*Top: Dutch TTF daily log-returns exhibiting volatility clustering. Bottom: Hamilton Markov Switching causal filtered probabilities $P(S_t = 1 \mid y_{1:t})$ (real-time, zero look-ahead bias) versus full-sample smoothed probabilities $P(S_t = 1 \mid y_{1:T})$.*

![Figure 2: Markov Switching Filtered vs Smoothed Probabilities](output/fig2_regime_smoothed_probabilities.png)

---

### Figure 3: Bayesian Structural Marginal Storage Elasticity Posteriors
*NumPyro No-U-Turn Sampler (NUTS) posterior distributions for the marginal storage elasticity parameter in Tranquil/Normal ($\beta_{\text{normal}}$) vs. Crisis ($\beta_{\text{crisis}}$) market regimes, showing non-overlapping 95% Highest Density Intervals (HDIs).*

![Figure 3: Bayesian Storage Elasticity Posteriors](output/fig3_bayesian_posterior_storage_effect.png)

---

### Figure 4: SHAP Feature Importance Decomposed by Market Regime
*Game-theoretic SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) values from LightGBM stratified across Normal (Low-Vol) and Crisis (High-Vol) regimes, demonstrating how physical inventory deviations and winter deadline proximity surge in predictive impact during supply squeezes.*

![Figure 4: SHAP Feature Importance Across Regimes](output/fig4_shap_regime_decomposition.png)

---

### Figure 5: STL Decomposition (Trend, Seasonality, Residuals)
*Seasonal-Trend decomposition using LOESS (STL) for EU aggregate gas storage fill percentage (left) and Dutch TTF log wholesale prices (right), separating seasonal co-movement from structural supply shocks.*

![Figure 5: STL Decomposition of Storage & TTF Price](output/fig5_stl_decomposition.png)

---

### Figure 6: Cross-Market Comparison (European TTF vs. US Henry Hub)
*Left: Price trajectory comparison in equivalent EUR/MWh. Right: Price-storage deviation convexity scatter, illustrating the severe upward convexity of European prices compared to US domestic shale flexibility.*

![Figure 6: European TTF vs US Henry Hub Comparison](output/fig6_cross_market_comparison.png)
